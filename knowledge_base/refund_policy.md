# SupportIQ Store — Returns, Replacements and Refunds Policy

> Demonstration policy for the fictional SupportIQ Store. Eligibility depends on product type, delivery date, and condition.

## How do I return an item or request a refund?
Open **My Orders**, choose the delivered item, select **Return or Replace**, and follow the displayed instructions. Standard eligible items may be returned **within 30 calendar days of delivery** if unused and in original condition with accessories and packaging. An agent must not approve a refund without checking order eligibility.

## Clothing, shoes and standard products
Eligible shoes, clothing and standard non-electronic items may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**. Items must be unused, with original labels, accessories, and packaging. The returns team checks the condition before approving a refund. If a return option is not available, contact human support for eligibility review.

## Electronics and appliances
Eligible electronics and appliances, including blenders, follow a **7-calendar-day replacement window from delivery** for items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. Replacement is the default remedy after verification. A refund may be considered by human support if an eligible replacement is unavailable. The AI cannot guarantee a replacement or refund.

## Damaged, defective or incorrect deliveries
If an item arrived damaged, defective, missing parts, or incorrect, report the issue through **My Orders → Return or Replace** as soon as possible and within the applicable return or replacement window. Keep the packaging and provide photos if requested by the returns team. Human support reviews evidence and confirms the remedy. Do not instruct customers to share payment card details, passwords, or OTPs.

## Non-returnable items
Opened personal-care products, perishable groceries, digital downloads, and gift cards are generally non-returnable, except where the item is defective, incorrect, or applicable consumer law requires a remedy. Exceptions require human review.

## Refund processing and payment timelines
After the return is received and approved, SupportIQ Store initiates the refund within **3 business days**. For card, UPI, and net-banking payments, banks typically take another **5–7 business days** to reflect the credit. Refunds for cash-on-delivery orders require a verified refund destination arranged by the returns team. These are estimates, not proof that a particular refund was issued.

## Refund not received
Check **My Orders → Return or Refund Status**. If the displayed refund initiation date is more than **7 business days** ago and funds have not arrived, request a human billing investigation. The AI must not invent a refund reference number, transaction status, or bank settlement date.

## Cancellation and shipping fees
An order may be cancelled from **My Orders** before dispatch if the cancellation option is available. After dispatch, use the return process if eligible. Original shipping fees are generally non-refundable unless the order was incorrect, damaged on arrival, or a human agent approves an exception under the shipping policy.
