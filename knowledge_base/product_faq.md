# SupportIQ Store — Product FAQ and Troubleshooting

> Demonstration FAQ for the fictional SupportIQ Store. Product-specific specifications must be checked against the actual listing.

## My blender arrived damaged. Can I get a replacement?
Yes, **eligible blenders and other appliances may be considered for replacement within 7 calendar days of delivery** if they arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. Open **My Orders → Return or Replace**, select the item, and report the damage. Keep the packaging and provide photos if requested. The returns team must verify eligibility before approving the replacement. If a replacement is unavailable, human support may review refund eligibility. See `refund_policy.md` for the full policy.

## My product is missing a part or accessory
Open **My Orders**, select the item, and report **Missing Parts** through the support option if available. Keep the packaging and item details. Human support will confirm whether a replacement part, full replacement, or other remedy is appropriate. The AI must not promise that a part has already been shipped.

## How do I request warranty service?
Open **My Orders** and download the invoice, if available. Check the product listing or manufacturer warranty documentation for the warranty period and covered defects. Contact human support through the in-app **Help or Support** option if you need assistance. Warranty duration and service-provider details vary by product and must not be invented.

## Is a product compatible with my device or power outlet?
Compatibility depends on the exact model and product specifications. Check the product listing, model number, supported voltage, connector type, operating system, and manufacturer instructions. The AI must not assume that every appliance includes an Indian plug adapter or that every device supports Indian 5G networks.

## Is cash on delivery available?
Cash on delivery may be offered for eligible products and delivery locations. Availability is shown at checkout. The AI cannot confirm COD availability for a specific postal code without checkout data.

## How can I track my product delivery?
Open **My Orders → Track Package**. For late deliveries, see `shipping_policy.md`. The AI cannot access live courier tracking unless a tracking integration is connected.

## Can I return an item if I changed my mind?
Eligible non-electronic items, such as shoes and clothing, may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery** if unused and in original condition. Electronics and appliances follow the separate **7-day replacement-only** rule for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. See `refund_policy.md`.

## Product safety concerns
If a device overheats, sparks, leaks, or appears unsafe, stop using it, disconnect power if safe to do so, and request urgent human support. Do not provide speculative repair instructions.
