# SupportIQ Store — Account and Login Policy

> Demonstration policy for the fictional SupportIQ Store.

## Forgot password: reset instructions
If you forgot your password or cannot log in, open the SupportIQ Store login page and select **Forgot Password**. Enter the email address registered to your account. Follow the secure reset link sent to that address. Password reset links **expire after 30 minutes**. If the link expires, request a new one.

## Password reset email not received
Check spam or junk folders and confirm that the registered email address was entered correctly. Allow a few minutes for delivery, then request a new reset link. If the email still does not arrive, contact human support. Never share your password or OTP with support staff.

## Account locked
If your account remains locked after a password reset, contact human support for identity verification. The AI cannot unlock accounts or change account ownership directly.

## Suspected unauthorized access
If you suspect someone accessed your account without permission, change your password using the official reset process and immediately request escalation to the **security support team**. If you still have access, review recent account activity. The AI should treat suspected compromise as a **high-priority human escalation**.

## Security and privacy rules
Never request or collect passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), full payment card numbers, CVVs, or bank login credentials. Support agents must not ask customers to send identity documents in the chat. Identity verification, where required, is performed only through an approved secure support process.

## Update account information
If available, update your contact details under **Account Settings**. Changes to a registered email address or account ownership that require identity verification must be handled by human support.
