# SupportIQ Store — Shipping and Delivery Policy

> Demonstration policy for the fictional SupportIQ Store. This document does not provide live order tracking.

## Delayed orders: how do I find out when my order will arrive?
If your order is delayed or has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, sign in to SupportIQ Store, open **My Orders**, select the order, and check **Track Package** for its latest status and updated estimated delivery date, if available. A customer-support agent can explain these steps but **cannot confirm an exact arrival date without live order tracking data**. If tracking has not updated for **48 hours**, or the estimated delivery date has passed, request a delivery investigation from human support. Do not promise a specific delivery date.

## Delivery estimates
Standard shipping typically takes **3–7 business days after dispatch**, depending on destination and stock availability. Express delivery, when offered at checkout, typically takes **1–2 business days after dispatch**. These are general estimates, not guarantees. The checkout confirmation and the order's tracking page provide the applicable estimate for that order. Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays.

## Tracking an order
1. Sign in to SupportIQ Store.
2. Select **My Orders** and choose the relevant order.
3. Open **Track Package** to see dispatch status, courier updates, and the current estimated delivery date if provided.
4. If the package is marked delivered but you have not received it, check with household members or the building reception and contact human support promptly.

## Shipping fees
Standard shipping costs **₹40 per order** for orders under **₹499**. Standard shipping is free on eligible orders of **₹499 or more**. Express shipping, when offered, costs **₹79 per order**. The final shipping fee is displayed at checkout. Membership-based free-shipping programs are not part of this demonstration policy.

## Missed delivery and escalation
If the estimated delivery date has passed, or tracking has not updated for 48 hours, human support should investigate with the delivery partner. If a guaranteed delivery service was explicitly purchased and missed, human support must verify eligibility before confirming any delivery-fee reimbursement. The AI must not claim that a refund or replacement has already been approved.

## Missing or incorrect address
Customers may request an address correction through **My Orders** before dispatch, if the order interface allows it. After dispatch, address changes require human support and may not be possible.
